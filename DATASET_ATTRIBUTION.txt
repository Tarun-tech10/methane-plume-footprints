Changes made in this redistribution
-----------------------------------
Airborne MethaneAIR scenes only; satellite and simulated subsets excluded. Scenes without a flight
identifier and scenes not exactly 672 x 672 excluded. Float arrays converted to 16-bit PNG under the
global affine map ppb = 1000.0 + 0.1556 * code, with code 65535 reserved for a missing retrieval.
Scene and flight identifiers replaced by opaque digests; original filenames, timestamps, coordinates
and processing strings removed. Model archives and cross-validation fold files not included.
